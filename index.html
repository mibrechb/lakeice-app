Welcome to the Lake Ice Explorer

The Lake Ice Explorer is an interactive mapping application for exploring multi-sensor satellite observations of lake ice derived with deep-learning image classification.

Data preview Lake-ice outputs are currently being ingested and availability will change as processing continues. Lakes shown in grey do not currently have lake-ice results available.

This tool was developed as part of the SOS-Water EU Horizon project. Visit sos-water.eu to learn more about the project.

Explore lake ice cover and phenology across the European Alps. Select a lake directly from the map, jump to a featured lake, or learn how the lake ice observations and metrics are derived.
